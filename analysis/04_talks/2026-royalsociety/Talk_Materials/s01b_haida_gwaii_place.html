Haida Gwaii

At the edge of the North Pacific
A fragmented archipelago ~100 km off British Columbia. Graham in the north, Moresby in the south.
Deep Pacific water bathes hundreds of sheltered spawning coves along the east coast — a herring network running for ~10,700 years.
A geography built for spatial structure.
