## Thesis (revised)

> Haida Gwaii herring is a **coupled social–ecological tipping point**: an
> ocean regime shift that did not reverse when fishing stopped, empirically
> coupled to a collapse in the place-based knowledge/governance that once held
> the system's structure. No single mechanism explains the ecological side —
> and that non-identifiability *is* the tipping-point signature. The rational
> response is not better forecasts but governance matched to the system's real
> scale.

## Theory spine (the forum's own language — new in v2)

- **Lenton et al. 2008** (tipping elements) + Lenton's positive-tipping
  keynote → frame open and close in his vocabulary.
- **Scheffer 2001/2009** → resilience loss / hysteresis / why removing the
  driver need not reverse the state.
- **Rocha et al. 2018 *Science* (Levin co-author)** → the formal backbone of
  the coupling slide: *domino effects* (one-way) vs *hidden feedbacks*
  (two-way). Our claim = a hidden-feedback social–ecological cascade.
