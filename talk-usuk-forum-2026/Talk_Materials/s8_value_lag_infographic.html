The price held while the system fell

A demand shock from Japan made herring valuable; that value stayed high into the mid-1990s — even as the spatial structure had already eroded. The economic signal lagged the ecological one, and the market that created the fishery is not coming back.

The value signal lagged the ecosystem — herring stayed valuable while the structure that produced it was already gone. And the demand that created the fishery has structurally faded, so recovery must be governed, not waited for.
