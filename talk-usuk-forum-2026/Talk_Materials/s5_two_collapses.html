Coupled tipping points | Pacific herring | Haida Gwaii

Two collapses, two outcomes

The same fish, the same coast — fished two different ways. One collapse rebounded within about five years. The next has not come back in twenty-five.

Curve schematic (qualitative shape from the talk's vetted timeline + DFO sources), not a digitized series. Reduction fishery reached HG in the 1930s; catches peaked ~77,500 t in 1956; 1960s collapse rebounded within ~5 yr of closure. Roe fishery from 1972; by 1993 the fleet found ~1,000 t; closure 1994; ~25 yr on, still ~10% of historic (current m1_stier_11 / DFO SR 2025/005 — below LRP, 0-t). Two harvest modes: commercial vessels kill adults for roe; Haida k'aaw takes eggs after spawning, fish left alive.
