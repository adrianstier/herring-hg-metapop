## The argument in one frame

**Thesis.** Ecological tipping points and ecosystem-service tipping points are
empirically separable. Services have their own breakpoints that lead, lag, or
decouple from ecological state change. The gap between them is the interval in
which management still has leverage.

**Three layers.** Ecological state → biophysical service delivery → economic
and social value. Each layer tips on its own schedule. Hysteresis makes the
gaps consequential: a recovered ecological state does not retrace its path back
up through the service and value layers.

**Three structural predictions** (reconstructed from the separability thesis —
confirm wording against the finalized abstract):
1. **Separability** — service-delivery breakpoints are not coincident with
   ecological-state breakpoints; they lead, lag, or decouple.
2. **Hysteresis** — recovery of ecological state does not restore service
   delivery along the collapse path; service recovery lags and may not occur
   on management-relevant timescales.
3. **Management window** — the temporal offset between layer breakpoints is a
   measurable interval; the herring case shows the system after that window
   has closed.

## The four dynamic builds (built in this environment, timeline visual language; event-driven, advance on click)

**BUILD #1 — three-layer spine (2, 8, 15).** One shared time axis 1950→present,
three trajectories drawn in sequence. S2: axis + empty tracks. S8: ecological
curve joined by value curve (1993 peak → ~decade-later collapse); horizontal
gap = the management window, shaded. S15: cultural curve completes (flat across
baseline, late drop); all three breakpoints at different x; window shades shut.
One SVG, step index advances each return. Wire-in: synchrony series (Stier
2020) ecological track; coastwide landed value 1985–2010 value track;
schematic cultural track anchored to ~2022 closure.

**BUILD #2 — draggable hysteresis (10).** Phase portrait: x = driver
(predation pressure / cumulative mortality), y = system state, folded curve
(upper/lower stable branches, unstable middle). Drag driver right → dot falls
off the fold (collapse). Drag back left → dot does NOT retrace; stuck on lower
branch. Caption: *the driver is back where it started; the system is not.* Can
live inside the Build #1 artifact as the mechanism layer.

**BUILD #3 — portfolio comes apart (5).** Nine subpopulation biomass lines
fanned (independent) in early decades; on click time runs, post-1994 lines
braid into one strand; asynchrony index 1→0 counter ticks down. Wire-in: nine
focal subpop series + asynchrony index, 65-yr span. Annotations on reveal:
2.1× more stable than homogeneous metapop; local harvest 65% vs archipelago 4%.

**BUILD #4 — a bigger mouth is not a coefficient (7).** Year scrubber
1970→2025; stacked pressure/demand context updates live. Commercial removals
dominate early, then predator demand becomes a large ecological pressure field.
Wire-in: Doherty 2025 as a **WCVI** proof that mammal predation can dominate
natural mortality; HG predator-repo demand as the local scale context; Surma &
Pitcher 2015 and the current model ledger as the guardrail. Caption:
*large enough to matter, not yet identified enough to promote.*

## Charts (static, one idea, minimal ink)

- **S4 — 1967 collapse.** Coastwide catch 1935–1972 (climb to ~240 kt;
  241→181→135 kt fall; shaded 1968–71 closure). One callout: four leading
  indicators present and ignored (spatial contraction, rising search effort,
  reduced spawn, truncated age structure). Quiet setup slide.
- **S6 — what can and cannot explain it.** Mechanism-menu panel: spatial /
  portfolio erosion is the robust HG pattern; historical fishing is the
  cleanest descriptive recovery axis; PDO and predators remain plausible but
  confounded; density dependence and simple predator-pit stories are weak for
  HG. Transition slide. Source the ranking from the model ledger, covariate
  readiness registry, and `docs/herring-non-recovery-hypotheses.md`.
- **S8 — biomass vs. value (keystone, fused with SPINE).** Dual-axis 1985–2010;
  value peaks at 1993 ~$40M while metapop already failing, collapses ~93% by
  2006. Horizontal offset = Prediction 1 visible. Line: *aggregate landed
  value is the last thing to fall.*
- **S13 — Strait of Georgia counterfactual.** Two-column matched-row panel
  (HG vs SoG): stock status, current biomass (~100,000+ t SoG), residency vs
  migration, predation structure, fishery history. The negative control —
  immunizes against "you just picked a collapsed stock."
- **S14 — zero, still below the line.** 2025 forecast as probability
  distribution; LRP vertical rule; 37.8%-below-LRP mass shaded even with no
  fishing. Small panel: management procedures incl. "no fishing" as
  overlapping distributions, none clearing LRP. Source: Cleary 2024 / DFO CSAS
  SR 2025/005.
- **Backup — egg vs adult harvest asymmetry.** Operating-space diagram (egg vs
  adult harvest, depletion contour; "fishery target" and "predator-protective"
  spaces overlapping) — Shelton et al. 2014. Economics bridge to Kubiszewski.
