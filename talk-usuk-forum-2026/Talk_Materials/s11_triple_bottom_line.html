Coupled tipping points | Pacific herring | Haida Gwaii

A system in a new state — the triple bottom line, hit

After two decades of closure the system has not returned — it has settled into an alternative stable state at about a tenth of its former abundance, and it struck all three bottom lines on three different clocks.

Ecosystem
the food web
Predators that depend on herring, and the halibut and salmon downstream of it. Spatial structure — the portfolio — eroded.
tipped ~1993 · the shift in the middle
People
Haida k'aaw & the sector
Roe-on-kelp can no longer be reliably gathered at sites that once thrived — the very places where grandparents passed oral history to grandchildren. The commercial sector lost its fishery.
tipped ~1990 · the slow erosion
Economy
landed value
HG roe landed value peaked in the 1980s, stayed high to the mid-1990s, then declined (2024 Rebuilding Plan, Figs 31/32; DFO fish slips).
tipped ~2005 · the fast crash
The economic crash was fast. The cultural erosion was slow. The ecological shift sat between them — ecological and service tipping points decoupled.
