# S20 Solution Payload

Slide: S20, "What changes Monday".

This is the close. Keep it practical and directly tied to the proof objects
shown earlier.

## Main Claim

If ecological state, service delivery, and value tip on different clocks,
management has to protect structure before tonnage, manage exposure before a
single mechanism is proven, and build the missing data spine while decisions
are still live.

## Three Slide Lines

1. Measure structure, not just biomass.
2. Manage exposure under uncertainty.
3. Build the missing data spine.

## Spoken Payload

Measure structure, not just biomass:

- Report the 11-section fit and focal-9 sensitivity together.
- Track synchrony, occupied sections, top-three biomass share, and Simpson
  effective sections as management indicators.
- Treat concentrated biomass as a loss of insurance, not a clean recovery.

Manage exposure under uncertainty:

- No-fishing is necessary but has not been sufficient in Haida Gwaii.
- Historical fishing pressure is the strongest section-level recovery axis, but
  predators, climate, habitat/substrate, and survey access remain plausible
  coupled pressures.
- Manage cumulative exposure and preserve spatial portfolio structure while the
  mechanism work continues.

Build the missing data spine:

- Acquire source-traceable DFO biological input tables for catch-at-age,
  age/length, weight-at-age, and SCA recruitment inputs.
- Build section-year predator exposure products, especially a humpback spatial
  exposure layer that can be compared with seal and sea-lion exposure.
- Add survey effort/access metadata for Cumshewa, Louscoone, Skidegate, and
  other mechanism focal sections.

## Closing Sentence

"The transferable lesson is not that Haida Gwaii proves one mechanism. It is
that by the time biomass, service delivery, value, and governance all agree,
the management window has usually already closed."
