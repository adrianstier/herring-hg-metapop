## ABT spine (the narrative the slides must serve)

- **AND** — Herring fed Haida Gwaii for ~10,000 years; *both* people and
  predators built their lives around the same spring event, and we can now
  measure it precisely.
- **BUT** — one collapse never came back. Not from one dramatic flip into a
  new stable state, but because the spatial portfolio was slowly **drawn
  down** — cove by cove, age by age, decade on decade — and never rebuilt.
  "A new stable state" is *one* explanation among several; none is proven.
- **THEREFORE** — judge recovery against the system we actually have, and fix
  the *slow* drivers — **where, how, and at what age** we take herring —
  through the social-ecological system that governs it (Haida Nation · DFO/
  Canada · BC · Parks Canada).

The load-bearing reframe vs. the prior spec: the "tipping point" is the
**slow erosion of spatial structure + sustained drawdown**, not a fast
biomass crash into a proven bistable basin. This is the more defensible *and*
more transferable story, and it aligns with `docs/talk-model-claim-control-
sheet.md` ("post-closure non-recovery does not identify a single promoted
mechanism"; "manage under uncertainty; do not wait for a single solved
mechanism").

## The Haida Gwaii food-web diagram ④ (spec)

Bespoke replacement for `s03b_foodweb_oceana.png`. Native build (pptxgenjs
shapes+text on the deck dark chrome) — robust, on-brand, no external rights.
Wasp-waist topology:

- **Inflow (bottom):** Haida Gwaii ocean productivity → phytoplankton →
  zooplankton.
- **The waist (single channel):** Pacific herring, spawning in HG coves.
- **Fan-out (top), HG-specific consumers (audited species where numeric):**
  marine mammals (humpback whale, Steller sea lion); groundfish/commercial
  fish (Pacific cod, halibut, lingcod, hake, sablefish); salmon (juvenile/
  recruitment *context* only — claim sheet); seabirds & shore (bald eagle,
  gulls); **terrestrial — black bear, wolf** (the HG-distinctive land–sea
  coupling: bears/wolves eat spawn on the beach); **people — Haida kʼaaw/FSC
  + the (now-closed) commercial fishery**.
- **Place identity:** Haida Gwaii archipelago silhouette / coastline accent;
  "Haida Gwaii" labelled. Caption: original diagram, this work.
- The land–sea link (bear/wolf/eagle on the spawn) is the deliberate
  differentiator from the generic Oceana diagram and carries ②.
